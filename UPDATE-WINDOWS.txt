SISSY FIGHTS NXT 1.1.0 — UPDATE AN EXISTING XAMPP GAME
=====================================================

1. Close the game in your browsers. Stop the bot worker with
   bin\stop-bots.cmd and wait until its console confirms the stop.
2. Back up your existing game folder and export its database using
   phpMyAdmin. Keep app\config.php: it contains your existing connection.
3. Stop Apache in XAMPP while copying the update.
4. Open the new archive, then copy the CONTENTS of its sissy-fights-nxt
   folder into your EXISTING C:\xampp\htdocs\sissy-fights-nxt folder.
   Allow replacement of matching files. Merge the folders; do not delete
   your existing folder. This release does not contain app\config.php,
   so your connection and installed state remain in place.
5. Start Apache again, then start the worker with bin\start-bots.cmd.
6. Open the normal game URL and press Ctrl+F5 once to refresh old assets.
   Open your profile to choose any of the 32 fighter looks.

Do not run the installer again and do not clear/recreate the database.
The automatic cosmetic update changes bot appearance IDs only. It keeps
human appearances, usernames, passwords, ratings, histories and records.
Fights already in progress retain their saved fighter choices and use the
original arena when they have no arena ID. New fights choose one of the
ten new arenas at creation, and keep it for the entire fight.

Sound starts enabled for a browser without a saved preference. If you
muted the old version, choose SOUND ON. The first click/tap or keypress
unlocks playback under the browser's normal autoplay rules.

NEW INSTALL? Follow README.txt instead. XAMPP/PHP/MySQL are still required.
